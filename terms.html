Terms of use

What you get, what it costs, and what we cannot promise about a mended photograph.

Free and Pro

Relumo may let you try a restoration free of charge; what is included without paying is spelled out in the app. Restoring the whole box needs Relumo Pro. Pro is sold as a weekly plan (with a three-day free trial for new subscribers who qualify) and as a yearly plan. The price in your currency and any trial offer are shown on the purchase screen before you commit.

How billing behaves

Apple collects payment through your Apple ID. Plans extend themselves for another equal term unless you disable renewal at least one day before the running term finishes. If you switch off renewal, access continues until the paid term ends. Manage it under iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Relumo. Removing the app is not the same as cancelling. Apple decides on refunds.

What a restoration is

The results come from automated models. They can invent texture, blur small details, change a facial expression slightly, or pick colours that never existed. A restored picture is an artistic reconstruction and should not be used as evidence, identification or a legal record. We do not guarantee a particular outcome.

Your pictures, your responsibility

Send only photos you own or have the right to restore, and never images that are unlawful, exploit anyone or invade a person's privacy. You keep all rights in your photos; you give us only the permission needed to process them to produce your result. Do not try to abuse, scrape or overload the restoration service.

Liability

Relumo is provided as is, and the server part can be slow or unavailable, including when a daily allowance is used up. Within what the law permits, we accept no responsibility for photos that are lost or changed, or for knock-on damage, so keep the originals and your own backups. Rights that the law gives you as a consumer remain in force. These terms are read under the law that applies where the publisher is established, without cutting back those protections.
