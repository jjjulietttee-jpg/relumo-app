Privacy, asked and answered

Plain questions that people put to us about their family photos, with the real answers.

The journey of one photo

  1. You choose. A new shot with the camera, or one picture you pick yourself. Relumo never browses your library.
  2. Done on the phone. Basic cleaning and tone fixes run on the device and need no connection.
  3. Only if you allow it. For faces and colour, a JPEG scaled to at most 1,600 pixels on its long side goes over an encrypted link to our restoration relay, a small cloud worker we operate.
  4. Handed to the engines. The relay passes the picture to fal.ai, which runs a face-repair model (CodeFormer) and, if you picked colour, a colourising model (DDColor). The result flows back the same way.
  5. Home again. The repaired picture is saved inside Relumo. Nothing goes to Photos until you tap Save.

Do you keep my photos?

Our relay does not store them: the picture is held in memory while the job runs and then dropped. The relay asks fal.ai to remove its own temporary copies after one hour. Beyond that, fal.ai's own rules govern what it does with files during that window, and we do not control them. We have no photo library of our customers and we do not look at what you send.

Why does the server see a number?

Each install makes a random identifier that is not linked to your name, Apple ID or contacts. It is sent with a job only so the relay can count how many restorations that install made today, because every server job costs us money. The counters are kept by day and expire after about two days. Reinstalling creates a new number.

Can I switch the server off?

Yes. In Settings the switch called AI restoration on our server is the permission. Relumo asks for it before the first upload. When it is off, nothing leaves the phone and only the on-device fixes are used.

Where are my results, and how do I erase them?

Restored photos live in a private folder inside the app, with a small record of each on the device. In Settings, Your photos tap Delete all restored photos and confirm with Delete all; individual photos have their own delete button. Copies you already saved to Photos stay there, because they are yours and outside the app. Deleting Relumo removes the rest.

What permissions does it ask for?

Camera to photograph a print. Photos, add only to save a finished picture when you tap Save. Choosing an existing picture goes through the iOS picker, which shares just that one. No location, no contacts, no microphone, no tracking prompt.

Who else is involved?

Apple for purchases. Adapty to see whether Pro is active and which offer to show; it holds an anonymous profile and purchase events, not photos. fal.ai and the relay described above for restoration. If you use Write to us in the app, the e-mail draft is pre-filled with the build number, your iOS release and your Adapty profile number, and you can delete them before sending.

Children and changes

Relumo is not aimed at children under 13. If we alter this page in a way that affects you, the revision date changes and the app points to the new text. For data requests, write to nastya.shchaveleva@internet.ru.
