Support

Quick fixes for the things people ask us, then how to reach a person.

Quick answers

My Pro disappeared after reinstalling.Go to Settings inside Relumo, press Restore purchases, and make sure the iPhone is logged into the Apple account that made the purchase.

Faces and colour are greyed out or ask permission.Turn on AI restoration on our server in Settings. Those two steps need it.

It says the daily limit is reached or the service is busy.The relay caps how many jobs one install, and the whole app, may run per day. Try again tomorrow, or later if it only says busy. Basic on-device fixes still work.

How do I clear my restored photos?Settings, Delete all restored photos, then Delete all. Anything you saved to Photos is untouched.

The result looks too smooth, or the face changed.Try a flatter, evenly lit shot with no glare, or restore without colour. Fine detail lost in the original cannot be recovered, so keep the print.

How do I cancel?On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name at the top, pick Subscriptions, select Relumo, then Cancel Subscription.
